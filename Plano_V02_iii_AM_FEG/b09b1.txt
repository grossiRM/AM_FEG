Ois an open source toolbox that is capable of considering both powder packing and molten pool modeling based on programming language C++. The physical processes of PBF-L of Ti-6Al-4V were simulated considering the surface tension, Marangoni flow, recoil pressure, drag force due to solid/liquid transition [150,186,194]. Powder particles were packed on the substrate before laser scanning [150,186]. A probability density function with Gaussian fit was used to simulate the size distribution of powder particles. Subsequently, a powder dropping model was used to calculate the random positions and arrangements of the powder particles in the powder bed [194]. An example of the simulated molten pool and powder particles using OpenFOAM is shown in Fig. 2.12(b) [186]. The evolution of the liquid/gas interface of the molten pool was simulated through the coupling of the Navier- Stokes equation, energy conservation, continuity equation and volume-of-fluid equation [150,186,19]
